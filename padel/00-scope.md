# Scope — Who Does What

You asked me to manage everything. Here is the honest boundary, because the parts I cannot do are the parts that keep a business legal and funded, and you should know about them before we start rather than at the moment they block us.

---

## What only you can do

Not preference — these are identity, money and legal accountability. No agent gets to hold them.

| | Why |
|---|---|
| **Register a business, handle VAT** | Legal identity. In the EU, selling goods requires it |
| **Open Stripe / a bank account** | KYC verification is you, with your documents |
| **Pay for anything** | Domain, ads, samples, stock. Your card, your decision |
| **Create the ad accounts** | Meta and TikTok hold a real person accountable for ad policy |
| **Send the supplier emails** | I draft them. You send them from your address and sign the terms |
| **Receive and ship the goods** | Someone with an address and hands |
| **Press "publish" and "spend"** | Every irreversible action is yours to approve |

**The practical shape of it:** I build everything up to the click. You click.

## The plan, staged by money

Nothing in a later stage happens until the earlier one passes. You said validate first — this is what that actually looks like.

### Stage 0 — Decide and build · €0 · me
Product decision, full brand, landing page built and deployed, ad creative, email flows, the economics model. All of it before a euro moves.

### Stage 1 — Prove it's real · €40–80 · you
- Domain (~€12/yr)
- 2–3 supplier sample orders (~€30–60)

The samples matter more than they sound. **You cannot sell a grip you have not held.** If it feels cheap in your hand, we learn that for €40 instead of €400.

### Stage 2 — Test demand · €200–300 · you
A pre-order or waitlist page and a real ad test. We are buying one piece of information: **will a padel player who has never heard of us give us an email address or a card?**

Pass or fail is defined before we spend, not after. I will write the kill criteria into the test plan so neither of us gets to move the goalposts.

### Stage 3 — Only if Stage 2 passes · €200–400 · you
First branded stock order. Private label, not dropshipping — see the product decision for why that distinction decides whether this is a business or a hobby.

**Total exposure before you know whether it works: about €350.** If Stage 2 fails, you have spent €350 and learned something true, and I will tell you plainly that it failed rather than proposing we test it eleven more ways.

## What I will not do

- Invent proof. Same rule as the last engagement: no fabricated reviews, no invented statistics, no fake scarcity, no testimonials from people who do not exist.
- Tell you it is working when it is not.
- Quote you a CPA before an impression has run. I will give you the ceiling and the kill criteria instead.
- Spend your money or commit you to anything without you clicking it yourself.
