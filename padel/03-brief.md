## The Creative Brief

### 1. Objective
Prove that regular padel players will give a new brand their email — and then their money — for grips. Stage 2 measures intent, not revenue.

### 2. The Audience
**The committed amateur.** Plays **2–4 times a week**, owns their own racket rather than renting, books on Playtomic, sits in two or three WhatsApp groups that exist only to organise games. They have opinions about rackets. They have never once had an opinion about a grip brand.

Not the social player who plays monthly — they burn one grip a year and are worth nothing to a subscription.

### 3. The Core Problem
**They know the grip is dead. They play on it anyway.**

An overgrip lasts **2–3 weeks** at that frequency. It goes shiny, loses tack, starts sliding — and it does this gradually, so there is never a moment that forces action. Replacing it means remembering, ordering, waiting. So they grip harder instead, which costs control and tires the forearm, and they tell themselves they will sort it out next week.

**The gap between knowing and having is the entire business.**

### 4. The Desired Outcome
Never think about grips again. Never play on a dead one.

### 5. The Offer
Provisional, pending supplier quotes: a multi-pack entry unit around €29 for 10, with subscription as the default — grips arriving on the wear cycle rather than on memory. A larger season pack tested alongside it, because the economics need a bigger first order than a three-pack can give.

### 6. The Single-Minded Message
> **You've been playing on a dead grip.**

### 7. The Reasons to Believe
All require verification before they appear in copy — same register discipline as the last engagement.

- **RTB-1** — Spec held to PU or PU blend, 0.6–0.8mm, 1100mm minimum. *Verifiable from the supplier's own sheet.*
- **RTB-2** — Tested by actual padel players before launch. *True only once it is done. Starts with your contact.*
- **RTB-3** — Delivery timed to the real wear cycle. *Verifiable from our own shipping.*
- **RTB-4** — Price per grip versus buying singles at a club. *Verifiable once prices are set.*

No performance claims. No "pro-grade." No invented endorsements.

### 8. The Call to Action
**Stage 2:** *Get first access* — email only, no payment, no company required.
**Stage 3:** *Get your first pack.*

### 9. Deliverables
Brand and name · packaging design · waitlist page · club outreach kit · organic content for Instagram and TikTok · email flow · the ad test creative.

### 10. Success Criteria — set before spending, not after

| Signal | Threshold | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per email, cold paid | **≤ €2.00** | Channel viable |
| Cost per email | €2–4 | Marginal. Organic and clubs carry it |
| Cost per email | **> €4.00** | **Paid is dead for this product.** Stop paid, go all-in on clubs |
| Total emails from €300 | **≥ 150** | Proceed to stock |
| Total emails from €300 | **< 40** | **The offer or the market is wrong. Stop.** |
| Club conversations | ≥ 3 clubs willing to stock or trial | Distribution is real |

**These numbers are fixed now so that neither of us gets to move them later.**

## The campaign in one sentence

> **For padel players who play often enough to wear grips out, this campaign makes a fresh grip feel like the difference between playing your game and managing your racket — because the grip has been dead for two weeks and they already know it.**
