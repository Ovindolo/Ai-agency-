# Phase 2 — The Creative Brief

**Client:** AIRING (agency-originated concept)
**Campaign:** Launch, Q1
**Prepared by:** Creative Director / Brand Strategist

---

## 1. The Objective

Acquire first-time buyers of the $34 Starter Kit through paid social, and establish AIRING as the brand that owns a single idea in garment care: **washing is the damage.** Secondary objective — convert a meaningful share of first purchases to refill subscription within 90 days, because the refill is the business.

## 2. The Audience

People who deliberately traded quantity for quality in their wardrobe and are now watching those clothes wear out anyway. 27–44, $90k+ household income, wool-and-denim-heavy closets, fluent in garment quality, already re-wearing their clothes but doing it furtively.

They are not laundry shoppers. They have never once walked the fabric-care aisle with intent. They are *wardrobe* shoppers who have not yet noticed that garment care is where their wardrobe is dying.

## 3. The Core Problem

They are trapped in a loop they cannot see. Good clothes feel dirty by day two, so they wash them. Washing is what degrades them. Degraded clothes get replaced. The behavior meant to protect the wardrobe is what is destroying it — and the machine responsible is the one appliance nobody has ever thought to question.

Underneath the money sits a quieter problem: re-wearing feels faintly shameful. Nobody has ever given them permission.

## 4. The Desired Outcome

A wardrobe that still looks new in year three. And the un-self-conscious confidence to wear the same thing again tomorrow.

## 5. The Offer

**AIRING Starter Kit — $34.** 250ml refillable aluminum bottle, one concentrate, choice of *Cold Air* or *Line Dry*. 30-day money-back guarantee. Refill 3-pack $27; subscribe and save 15%.

## 6. The Single-Minded Message

> **It's not the wearing that wears your clothes out. It's the washing.**

Tagline: **Wear it again.**

## 7. The Reasons to Believe

Each is stated here as it would appear in copy, with its evidence status. **None may run until its register entry clears.**

- **RTB-1** — Mechanical agitation, heat and repeated wet cycling drive pilling, shrinkage and fiber loss in knitwear. *Requires: cited textile-science literature, reviewed by counsel.*
- **RTB-2** — AIRING removes odor compounds rather than masking them, via enzyme action plus molecular capture. *Requires: formulation documentation + independent sensory odor panel.*
- **RTB-3** — No softeners, no quats, no residue. Safe on merino, cashmere, raw denim and elastane. *Requires: formulator sign-off + fabric compatibility testing on all four.*
- **RTB-4** — One bottle, refilled by concentrate. Verifiable product architecture. *Verifiable at launch — lowest-risk RTB and therefore the one that carries the most weight early.*
- **RTB-5** — Made without [final exclusion list]. *Requires: locked formula.*

Full detail in `claims-substantiation-register.md`.

## 8. The Call to Action

**Get the Starter Kit — $34.** One button, one destination, one price, everywhere.

## 9. The Required Deliverables

Paid social video and static · UGC scripts and shot lists · product photography direction and prompts · conversion landing page · seven-post organic launch campaign · brand lock and visual generation specification.

## 10. The Success Criteria

Commercial and diagnostic criteria are set by the client at media-plan stage; the agency does not set a CPA target it cannot be accountable to. What the creative is accountable for:

- **Comprehension** — an untrained viewer can restate the core message after one view of a 15-second ad. Tested with five-second and one-view comprehension checks before spend.
- **Distinctiveness** — the work is unmistakable against Febreze, Downy, Day Two, Steamery and The Laundress in a side-by-side of muted ads.
- **Consistency** — any asset pulled at random is identifiably AIRING without its logo.
- **Claim integrity** — zero unregistered claims reach production. Non-negotiable.

---

## The campaign in one sentence

> **For people who buy fewer, better clothes, this campaign makes the AIRING Starter Kit feel like the clearest path from watching $120 knitwear pill and sag within a season to a wardrobe that still looks new in year three — because the damage was never the wearing, it was the wash cycle, and AIRING removes the odor that sends clothes into it.**
