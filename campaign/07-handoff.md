## 8. Testing hypotheses

Three sequential questions. No outcome is predicted.

| Test | Question | Units | Read on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does the fiber-damage frame buy attention from a cold stranger? | V-01 vs. product-first control | Hook rate, 3-second hold |
| 2 | Which entry point — enemy, loss, knowledge, or confession? | H1/H2/H3/H4 cuts of V-01 | Hook rate → outbound CTR |
| 3 | Does leading with the objection convert the skeptic? | V-02 vs. V-01, warm retarget | ROAS in the 7-day pool |

Supporting: S-02 tests whether removing the product from the image improves click quality (read on add-to-cart, not CTR). S-03 tests whether audience-specific creative beats universal creative. V-03 tests non-verbal brand-build (read on branded search lift).

**Kill criteria and CPA targets are set with the media buyer against real spend floors.** The agency does not quote a target before an impression has run.

## 9. Recommended production order

**Weeks 1–2 — Foundation.** Trademark clearance. Formula lock. Commission RTB-1 literature review and RTB-2 odor panel *now* — they are the long pole and they gate 70% of the copy.
**Week 2 — References.** Produce the physical bottle. Shoot it properly. Render the brand board. Freeze both. **Nothing generative starts before these two files exist**, or every asset drifts and the drift is unfixable later.
**Week 3 — Evidence photography.** PS-03 first. It is the campaign's load-bearing image; if it fails, the direction needs re-testing before more money goes in.
**Week 4 — Studio and lifestyle.** PS-01, PS-02, the commissioned list.
**Week 4 — Page build.** In parallel. Ship with `[RTB-n]` blockers live so the build physically cannot deploy early.
**Week 5 — Video.** V-01 first, then UGC across three casting variants, then V-02 and V-03. The animatic is the edit reference: match its pace and its audio cut at 0:07 before deviating.
**Week 6 — Legal.** Full claim review against the register. Expect cuts. Budget for a copy pass after them, not before.
**Week 7 — Organic first, paid second.** Social posts 1–5 run before any spend, so the paid traffic lands on a profile with a position rather than an empty grid.
**Week 8 — Test 1 only.** One question at a time.

## 10. Items requiring human approval

**Blocking — nothing ships until these clear**

1. **Trademark and domain clearance on AIRING (A-10).** The name is unverified. Every asset in this package carries it. This is the highest-risk item in the engagement.
2. **RTB-1, RTB-2, RTB-3 substantiation.** ~70% of the copy is unpublishable until these clear. See the register.
3. **The Phase 3 approval gate was self-cleared.** The client instructed the agency to run all phases and the client is also the agency here. A human must confirm Direction A. If B or C is preferred, Phases 4–7 are rebuilt, not edited.
4. **Offer terms (RTB-7, RTB-8).** Pricing, the 30-day guarantee, the $30 shipping threshold — all invented by the agency, all commercial decisions the agency cannot make.
5. **Formulation feasibility (A-04).** No chemist has seen this. If the mechanism is not achievable at this price, Direction A loses its argument and the B-led fallback applies.

**Also required before launch**

5b. **A five-touch post-purchase flow** (F-14). 46% of modeled twelve-month value arrives after the ad works, and the campaign originally specified nothing to earn it. Spec in `08-unit-economics.md`. This is a deliverable, not an optimization.

**Approval before action**

6. Any media spend. 7. Any publishing to any platform. 8. Any creator or supplier contact. 9. Any domain purchase or store build. 10. Synthetic-media labeling sign-off for all UGC under Meta and TikTok policy.
