AIRING Brand Board

The permanent BRAND REF for every asset in the campaign — the mark, the palette and its usage limits, the type system, and the product spec that governs all four generation prompts. Below it, the three static ads laid out at 4:5 so the type can be judged in frame.

The mark

One word, one line, one color.

Airing

Construction

Archivo, weight 600, width axis 122, all caps, letterspaced +9%. Single line. No icon, no symbol, no lockup variant, no tagline attached.

Color

One color only. Graphite on light grounds, Paper on dark. Never tinted, never reversed out of an image without a solid pad behind it.

Limits

Minimum 18mm print, 72px screen. Clear space on all sides equal to the cap height. Never curved, stretched, outlined or duplicated.

Palette

Four values. One of them is a scalpel.

Paper

#F4F2ED

Ground. Wardrobe, fabric, product body. 60–80% of frame.

Graphite

#1A1A18

Type, the machine, shadow. 15–30% of frame.

Line-Dry Blue

#2E4A7D

Denim, depth, the one saturated note. Under 20%.

Caution Orange

#E8541F

Evidence marks and CTA only. Under 5%.

Maximum share of any single frame

Paper 70 Graphite 22 Blue 6 Orange 2

Caution Orange is the only rule here that gets enforced rather than judged. If it is decorating, it is wrong — it points at damage, or it marks the CTA, or it is not in the frame.

Typography

One grotesk. One mono. No serif, ever.

The category default is a soft serif on cream. Refusing it is not a style preference — the entire visual position depends on reading as an instrument rather than a linen closet.

Forbidden

What never appears, in any asset.

Product

  • Rounded shoulder or altered bottle shape
  • Pistol-grip trigger or squeeze head
  • Any color but Paper White body, black pump
  • Invented label copy, icons, certification badges
  • Aerosol cloud instead of a fine pump fan

Claim

  • Anything not in the substantiation register
  • Antimicrobial, sanitizing, "kills 99.9%"
  • "Eco", "clean", "non-toxic" as bare adjectives
  • Any number without a registered source
  • "Replaces washing" without the limiting clause

Proof

  • Testimonials, ratings, review counts
  • Customer photos, press logos, "as seen in"
  • Awards, certifications, partnerships
  • Sales figures, waitlist numbers

Visual

  • Any serif, script or handwriting face
  • Sage, lavender, terracotta, cream
  • Steam, bubbles, suds, water droplets
  • Florals, botanicals, eucalyptus
  • Lens flare, sparkles, freshness swooshes
  • Centered symmetrical flat-lays on pure white

Concept work. AIRING is an agency-originated product concept. No bottle has been manufactured, no formula mixed, no photograph taken and no ad run.

The two canvas panels in the ad comps are drawn procedurally as art-direction stand-ins. They are layout placeholders for photographs that do not exist yet — not photographs, and not evidence. The third comp is a marked holder carrying its shot spec.

Claim markers on each unit name the evidence that must clear before that ad can run. All three are currently unrunnable.
