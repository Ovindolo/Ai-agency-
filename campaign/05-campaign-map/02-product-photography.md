# 3. Product Photography — Three Directions

All three share the standing block from `04-brand-lock.md`. What changes is the subject, the scene and the argument each image makes.

---

## PS-01 — Clean Studio Hero
### "The Instrument"

**The argument.** This is not a laundry product. It is a precision instrument that acts on fabric. It belongs next to a good knife or a light meter, not next to a bottle of softener.

**Composition.** Bottle standing, positioned on the right third, wordmark square to camera. Surface line at the lower quarter. Shadow traveling hard to the right and out of frame, becoming a graphic element in its own right. Upper-left 40% reserved empty for headline type. Nothing else in frame.

**Lighting.** Single hard source, camera left, 40° elevation, 45° azimuth. Defined shadow edge — a small strip softbox or a hard source with a grid, not a large diffusion panel. One black flag camera right to keep the shadow side reading dark and hold the aluminum's edge. No fill card on the front.

**Camera treatment.** 85mm, f/8, focus on the wordmark, tripod, eye-level with the wordmark so the bottle reads straight-on with no keystone.

**Surface.** Warm Paper White seamless paper — *paper*, not plastic, with its grain readable. Not pure white; pure white is the category default and is forbidden.

**Protected.** Bottle exactly per PRODUCT REF. Wordmark horizontal at the vertical midpoint, mono scent name below.

**Generation prompt.**
```
Studio product photograph, 4:5 vertical. A single 250ml cylindrical matte anodized
aluminum spray bottle in warm Paper White (#F4F2ED) stands upright on warm
paper-white seamless, positioned on the right third of the frame. Hard 90° flat
shoulder transitioning to the neck. Matte black fine-mist pump actuator and black
collar. The wordmark "AIRING" is screen-printed in dark Graphite (#1A1A18), all caps,
letterspaced, on a single horizontal line at the bottle's vertical midpoint, squarely
facing camera; a small monospaced scent name sits below it. One hard light source
from camera left at 40° elevation casts a single defined-edge shadow traveling right
across the paper and out of frame. Shadow side falls to deep neutral gray; matte
aluminum shows a soft specular roll-off and fine brushed grain, never mirror-like.
Surface line sits at the lower quarter. Upper-left 40% of the frame is empty negative
space. Cool-neutral 5200K, desaturated, fine film grain.
[STANDING BLOCK — 85mm, f/8, tripod, eye-level to the wordmark]
NEGATIVE — no second bottle, no reflection of a second bottle, no mirror finish, no
water, no steam, no mist, no droplets, no sparkle, no lens flare, no bokeh, no
gradient background, no pure white, no sage or lavender or cream, no florals or
botanicals, no props, no hands, no readable text other than "AIRING" and the scent
name, no invented label copy, no badges or certification icons, no warped or
duplicated geometry, no drop shadow on type, no watermark.
```

## PS-02 — Lifestyle Use Case
### "Day Two"

**The argument.** This lives in the five feet between the wardrobe and the door. It is part of getting dressed, not part of cleaning.

**Composition.** Horizontal 4:5. A Paper White merino tee on a wooden hanger hooked over the top edge of an open wardrobe door, occupying the left two-thirds. The bottle on the dresser top lower-right, smaller in frame, sharp, wordmark legible. Morning shadow from a window falls diagonally across both. Human presence implied and absent — a watch and two coins on the dresser, nothing else.

**Lighting.** Hard window light, camera left, low morning angle, throwing a window-bar shadow across the wall and the shirt. Deep unlifted shadow. No fill.

**Camera treatment.** 50mm, f/4, eye-level, handheld-feel but level. Shallow enough that the wall behind the hanger softens, deep enough that the wordmark holds.

**Surface.** Oiled wood dresser top with visible grain. Matte painted wall in Paper. A single Line-Dry Blue element — the jeans folded on the dresser corner — carrying the only saturation in frame.

**Protected.** Bottle per PRODUCT REF; garment carries no visible brand marks or labels.

**Generation prompt.**
```
Lifestyle interior photograph, 4:5. Early morning in a real bedroom. A Paper White
(#F4F2ED) merino wool t-shirt hangs from a wooden hanger hooked over the top edge of
an open wardrobe door, filling the left two-thirds of the frame; the knit structure
and a soft drape fold are clearly resolved. On the oiled-wood dresser top in the
lower-right, sharp and smaller in frame, stands the 250ml matte Paper White aluminum
AIRING bottle — hard 90° flat shoulder, black matte fine-mist pump actuator and collar,
"AIRING" wordmark in Graphite on a single horizontal line at the vertical midpoint,
monospaced scent name below, facing camera. A wristwatch and two coins sit beside it.
Folded indigo denim in Line-Dry Blue (#2E4A7D) at the dresser's far corner is the only
saturated note. Hard low window light from camera left throws a defined window-bar
shadow diagonally across the wall and the shirt; shadows stay deep and unlifted.
Matte Paper-colored wall. Cool-neutral 5200K, desaturated, fine grain.
[STANDING BLOCK — 50mm, f/4, eye-level, level horizon]
NEGATIVE — no people, no hands, no laundry basket, no folded towel stacks, no steam,
no water, no droplets, no plants, no florals or eucalyptus, no candles, no woven
baskets, no sage or lavender or terracotta or cream, no fairy lights, no bokeh balls,
no lens flare, no visible clothing labels or brand logos, no additional bottles, no
readable text other than the AIRING wordmark, no invented label copy, no warped
geometry, no watermark.
```

## PS-03 — Campaign Concept
### "The Evidence"

**The argument.** This is the campaign image. The one that makes the invisible benefit visible by photographing the *cost of the alternative*. It is the single most important shot in the package.

**Composition.** 1:1. Split frame. Left half: macro merino knit, heavily pilled, raked by hard light, every raised fiber ball reading as debris. Right half: the same fabric, intact, smooth, knit loops clean and ordered. The seam between them is a hard vertical line at dead center — the only permitted centered element in the system, because here the symmetry *is* the argument. A single Caution Orange mono annotation with a hairline leader points at one pill on the left half. **The annotation ships as `[RTB-1 — figure pending]` in the working file and is only ever set with a number the register has cleared.** If RTB-1 clears qualitatively but not numerically, the annotation reads `machine-washed` and the right half reads `not machine-washed` — which makes the same argument without a figure the brand cannot defend.

**Lighting.** One hard raking source, camera left, 20° elevation — almost skimming the surface, which is what makes pilling read at all. Identical light on both halves; the difference must be the fabric, never the lighting. No fill.

**Camera treatment.** 100mm macro, f/8, focus-stacked so the entire fabric plane holds. Perfectly perpendicular to the surface.

**Surface.** Merino knit at a scale where individual loops are legible — roughly 40mm of fabric across the frame.

**Protected.** No product in this image. Deliberately. The product appears in the next frame of the sequence, and withholding it here is what makes the image land as evidence rather than advertising.

**Generation prompt.**
```
Macro photograph, 1:1 square. A single continuous surface of Paper White (#F4F2ED)
merino wool knit fills the entire frame, roughly 40mm of fabric across. The left half
is heavily pilled and abraded: dozens of small raised fiber balls, lifted and broken
threads, a matted disordered surface. The right half is intact: clean ordered knit
loops, even tension, smooth. A hard vertical division runs down the exact center of
the frame. One single hard light source rakes from camera left at 20° elevation,
almost skimming the surface, identical across both halves, so that every raised fiber
catches a specular highlight and casts a tiny shadow. Shadows deep and unlifted.
Cool-neutral 5200K, heavily desaturated, fine film grain, extremely high fiber detail.
[STANDING BLOCK — 100mm macro, f/8 focus-stacked, sensor plane perfectly parallel
to the fabric]
NEGATIVE — no product, no bottle, no hands, no garment edges or seams or labels, no
text, no numbers, no annotation marks, no arrows, no water, no steam, no droplets,
no sparkle, no HDR halo, no over-sharpened micro-contrast ringing, no vignette, no
color cast, no blur gradient, no sage or cream, no watermark.
```
> Annotation and the Caution Orange leader line are added in layout, not generated. Generators cannot be trusted with legible type, and the whole image fails if the number renders as garbage.
