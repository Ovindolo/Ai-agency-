# Phase 5 — The Campaign Map

## 1. THE BIG IDEA

> **Your clothes aren't dying from being worn. They're dying from being washed — and AIRING is how you stop feeding them to the machine.**

**The line that carries it:** *Wear it again.*

**How every channel expresses the same idea**

| Channel | Its job in the argument |
|---|---|
| Paid video | Install the belief: the machine is the damage |
| Paid static | Hold the belief in a single frame of evidence |
| UGC | Make the belief personal — someone finds out and changes |
| Product photography | Make the product the instrument that acts on the belief |
| Landing page | Prove the belief, then resolve it with the offer |
| Organic social | Compound the belief into a body of knowledge |

If an asset does not advance that argument, it does not run.
