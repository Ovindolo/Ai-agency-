Craft notes

Three decisions you can see moving

Shots 1 and 2 are a match cut

Identical lattice, identical scale, identical push, hard cut between them. The only variable in the frame is the fabric. An earlier draft called for a whip transition — which would have destroyed the very comparison it was setting up, by giving the eye a reason to believe the two frames were shot differently.

24 frames, not 60

Motion is quantized to 24fps and the grain regenerates every frame. Smooth 60fps scaling of a static image reads as a slideshow pan; a stepped cadence with live grain reads as a moving image. It's the difference between a deck and a cut.

Captions invert, always

Graphite pad, Paper text, on every frame. The original spec said Paper pad — invisible on four of these five shots, all of which sit on a Paper ground. One inverting rule is legible everywhere and reads as a technical annotation, which is the brand's voice anyway.

The one orange mark

Shot 1 carries a single Caution Orange evidence mark on one pill, cut on at 0:01. That is the color doing its only permitted job: pointing at damage. It appears once in fifteen seconds.

Production

What must be shot, and what may be generated

Shots 1 and 2 must be photographed. They are the campaign's evidence. Generating the proof for a brand built on refusing fake proof is the worst available own-goal — the one place in this package where a synthetic asset is an integrity problem, not a production shortcut.

GenerateThe drum interior, V-02's fabric macros, all B-roll. None of it carries evidentiary weight.
Shoot orderBoth knit halves first, one session, light untouched between them. Then the drum. Then product interaction. The hanging-tee close reuses the PS-02 setup — same day.
Edit refMatch this animatic's pace and its audio cut at 0:07 before deviating. If a shot needs longer in the edit, take it from shot 4, never from the first three seconds.

V-02 — "The Objection" · 20s

Opens by conceding the category's weakness — "Fabric spray doesn't clean anything" — then earns its way back: what odor is, why masking fails, what enzymes do, and what the product does not do. Closes on the boundary line.

V-03 — "The Five Feet" · 12s

One continuous move through the five feet between the wardrobe and the front door. No voiceover. Sound design only: hinge, hanger, two pump hisses, a door closing.

The risk, plainly. A unit with no argument in it will underperform V-01 on direct response. Judge it on frequency-adjusted branded search lift — and kill it if that read isn't available.
